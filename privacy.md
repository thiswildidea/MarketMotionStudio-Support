# Privacy Policy — Market Motion Studio

**Effective 27 September 2026.**

## The short version

This application collects nothing. It has no analytics, no telemetry, and no
crash reporting service. Nothing about you or your use of the application is
sent to the developer or to any third party — not on first run, not on
failure, not ever.

The application talks to one kind of server: public stock-quote services, to
read market data for the chart you asked for. Everything it produces — the
videos you export — stays on your computer.

## What the application stores, and where

All of it lives in the application's own folder on your computer, under
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Packages\`. None of it is uploaded anywhere.

| What | Where | What it holds |
| --- | --- | --- |
| App settings | application settings storage | Your language, selected market (A-share / Hong Kong / US), tray-icon preference, and the folder you picked for exporting videos |
| `crash.log` | the application's local folder | Written only if the application fails unexpectedly; records the error type, never your data |
| Exported videos | the folder you chose | The MP4 files you exported, written only when you export |

There are no accounts, no sign-ins, and no passwords anywhere in the
application. It never asks for one and never stores one.

### About the logs

`crash.log` records exception types and stack traces — for example "XAML
System.NullReferenceException". It does not record anything you typed or any
market data. It is never transmitted. You can delete it at any time.

## What the application sends, and to whom

Only read-only requests for public market data, to the public quote services
the application uses (`qt.gtimg.cn`, `smartbox.gtimg.cn`,
`proxy.finance.qq.com`, `web.ifzq.gtimg.cn` — the Tencent Finance quote
service). A request carries a stock or index symbol — for example `sh000300` —
and nothing else: no identifier of yours, no device information, no usage
information.

The application sends nothing when you are not fetching data or exporting a
video. There is no other network destination. There is no developer server.

## What the developer can see

Nothing. There is no account, no registration, and no server to collect
anything. If you write to the support address below, the developer sees only
what you choose to put in that email.

## Children

The application is a market-data visualisation tool and is not directed at
children. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published at this address
with a new effective date.

## Contact

Questions about this policy, or about anything the application does with your
data: **gaqo@outlook.com**

When reporting a problem, please do not attach files from the application's
storage folder.

# 隐私政策 — Market Motion Studio

**生效日期：2026 年 9 月 27 日**

## 一句话版本

本应用**不收集任何数据**。没有分析统计，没有遥测，没有崩溃上报服务。关于你、你如何使用本应用的任何信息，都不会发送给开发者或任何第三方——首次运行时不会，出错时不会，任何时候都不会。

本应用只与一类服务器通信：公开行情服务，用于读取你请求的图表所需的行情数据。它产生的一切——你导出的视频——都留在你自己的电脑上。

## 本应用保存什么，保存在哪

全部位于本机 `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Packages\` 下应用自己的文件夹内，不会上传到任何地方。

| 内容 | 位置 | 说明 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 应用设置 | 应用设置存储 | 你选择的语言、市场（A股 / 港股 / 美股）、托盘图标偏好、以及你选定的视频导出文件夹 |
| `crash.log` | 应用本地文件夹 | 仅在应用意外失败时写入；只记录错误类型，不记录你的任何数据 |
| 导出的视频 | 你自己选择的文件夹 | 你导出时才写入的 MP4 文件 |

本应用**没有账号体系、没有登录、没有密码**。它从不索要，也从不保存。

### 关于日志

`crash.log` 只记录异常类型和堆栈——例如"XAML System.NullReferenceException"。它**不记录**你输入的任何内容，也不记录行情数据，且从不传输。你可以随时删除它。

## 本应用向谁发送什么

只向应用使用的公开行情服务（`qt.gtimg.cn`、`smartbox.gtimg.cn`、`proxy.finance.qq.com`、`web.ifzq.gtimg.cn`，即腾讯财经行情服务）发送**只读**的行情请求。请求中只携带股票或指数代码——例如 `sh000300`——不含其他任何内容：没有你的任何标识，没有设备信息，没有使用情况信息。

当你不在抓取数据或导出视频时，应用不发送任何内容。除此之外没有任何其他网络目的地，**不存在开发者服务器**。

## 开发者能看到什么

什么也看不到。本应用没有账号体系、无需注册，也不存在任何用于收集信息的服务器。如果你写信到下面的支持邮箱，开发者能看到的只有你自己写进那封邮件的内容。

## 儿童

本应用是行情数据可视化工具，并非面向儿童。它不向任何人收集信息，儿童亦然。

## 变更

若本政策发生变更，修订后的版本将发布在本网址，并标注新的生效日期。

## 联系方式

关于本政策，或关于本应用如何处理你的数据的任何疑问：**gaqo@outlook.com**

报告问题时，请**不要**附上应用存储文件夹里的文件。
