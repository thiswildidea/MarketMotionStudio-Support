# Market Motion Studio — Support

A Windows desktop application that turns market indicators into vertical
(9:16) animated videos: whole-market daily turnover, sector races, monthly
returns, a buy-and-hold position replayed day by day, a recurring investment
plan, a market-cap race — for the China A-share, Hong Kong and US markets,
with a fourth market of its own for cryptocurrency, which is offered outside
mainland China (both of its price sources sit abroad) — exported as H.264 MP4
ready to post to Douyin, Kuaishou, Xiaohongshu, WeChat Channels, TikTok, YouTube
and Instagram.

## Getting help

Email **gaqo@outlook.com**. It helps to include:

- what you were trying to do,
- what the application said, word for word,
- which market you had selected (A-share / Hong Kong / US, or cryptocurrency)
  and which stock, index or coin.

Please do not attach passwords or files from the application's storage folder.
None of those are needed to diagnose a problem — the application has no
accounts and no passwords, and its storage folder only holds your settings and
a local crash log.

The application ships with help in fourteen languages, reachable from the Help
page inside it. That is the fastest answer for most questions.

## The manual

The manual is published here as well as inside the application — one document
and its pictures per language, under [`help/`](help/). Pressing Help inside the
application reads the copy here first, so a correction made to this site reaches
the application the next time Help is opened rather than on the next release. If
it has no network, or this site is unreachable, the application shows the copy
shipped inside it instead: the manual opens either way, and the two are the same
files.

## Privacy

The application collects nothing — no analytics, no telemetry, no crash
reporting. It reads public market quotes over the network and writes the videos
you export to the folder you pick. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy) for what
it stores on your own computer and what it sends to whom.

## Reporting something that looks wrong

If a chart or a number looks wrong, include the stock or index symbol, the
market, and the date range you used. Market data arrives from a public quote
service; a wrong symbol or a trading holiday can look like a bug, and those
three details are the difference between a guess and a fix.

# Market Motion Studio — 支持

一款把行情指标做成竖屏（9:16）动画视频的 Windows 桌面应用：全市场成交额、行业板块竞速、月度收益、逐日盯市的持仓回放、定投计划、市值竞速——覆盖A股、港股、美股，另有加密货币这第四个市场（只在中国大陆以外提供，它的两个行情源都在境外）——导出为可直接发布到抖音、快手、小红书、视频号以及 TikTok、YouTube、Instagram 的 H.264 MP4。

## 获取帮助

发邮件至 **gaqo@outlook.com**。附上这些信息会更快：

- 你当时想做什么；
- 应用原话说了什么（请照抄）；
- 你选的是哪个市场（A股 / 港股 / 美股，或加密货币），以及哪只股票、指数或币种。

请**不要**附上密码或应用存储文件夹里的文件。诊断问题不需要这些——本应用没有账号体系、不存密码，存储文件夹里只有你的设置和一份本地崩溃日志。

应用内置了十四种语言的帮助文档，从应用里的"帮助"页面即可打开。大多数问题在那里能最快找到答案。

## 手册

手册除了随应用发布，也在这里发布一份——每种语言一份文档加它的配图，都在 [`help/`](help/) 下面。应用里打开"帮助"时先读这里的这份，所以在这里改一句话，下次打开帮助就能看到，不必等下一个版本；没有网络或这个站点连不上时，应用改用它自带的副本。两种情况下手册都能打开，而且是同一批文件。

## 隐私

本应用不收集任何数据——没有分析统计、没有遥测、没有崩溃上报。它通过网络读取公开行情，把你导出的视频写入你自己选择的文件夹。应用在你电脑上保存了什么、向谁发送了什么，详见[隐私政策](privacy)。

## 报告你认为不对的结果

如果某个图表或数字看起来不对，请附上股票或指数代码、市场、以及你使用的日期区间。行情数据来自公开行情服务；代码写错或遇上交易日休市都可能看起来像 bug，这三条信息是"猜测"和"修好"之间的区别。
